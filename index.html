Privacy Policy

Seafarer's Toolkit

Effective date: 3 October 2026

1. Introduction

Seafarer's Toolkit is a utility application designed for Merchant Navy cadets and seafarers. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

2. Information We Collect

The current version of Seafarer's Toolkit does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location in order to use its core tools.

3. Local App Settings

Certain preferences, including your selected theme and time format, are stored locally on your device so that the application can remember your choices between sessions.

4. Sea-Time Widget

Seafarer's Toolkit includes a Sea-Time home-screen widget. Information required to configure and display the widget may be stored locally on your device to maintain the widget and its selected settings.

5. Advertising

The current version of Seafarer's Toolkit does not include an advertising SDK. Advertising may be introduced in a future version of the application. If advertising services are added, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant third-party services and their data practices before that version is released.

6. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The current application dependencies do not include Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. If analytics or crash-reporting services are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

7. Third-Party Services

Future versions of the application may use third-party services to provide additional features, advertising, analytics, or other functionality. Where those services process user information, their use and relevant privacy practices will be disclosed in an updated Privacy Policy.

8. Data Security

Reasonable care is taken when designing the application and its data-handling features. However, no method of electronic storage or software system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when new features or third-party services are introduced or when the application's data practices change. The date displayed at the top of this page will be updated when the policy changes.
